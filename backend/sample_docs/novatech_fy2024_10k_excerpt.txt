ITEM 1 — BUSINESS

NovaTech Industries Inc. ("NovaTech" or "the Company") designs and sells industrial automation controllers, edge AI inference modules, and predictive-maintenance software to manufacturers in North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia. The Company was incorporated in Delaware in 2011 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas. As of December 31, 2024, NovaTech employed 4,820 full-time employees.

Principal product lines:
  - ControLogic Series (industrial PLCs and I/O)
  - EdgeMind Modules (on-prem AI inference appliances)
  - PulseCare Software (SaaS predictive maintenance)

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS — FISCAL 2024

Total net revenue for fiscal 2024 was $2.847 billion, an increase of 18.4% from $2.404 billion in fiscal 2023. Net income was $312.6 million, or $2.41 per diluted share, compared with $241.0 million, or $1.88 per diluted share, in fiscal 2023.

ITEM 1A — RISK FACTORS (SELECTED)

Supply concentration: Approximately 62% of ControLogic printed circuit assemblies are manufactured by two contract manufacturers in Penang, Malaysia. A prolonged outage could reduce ControLogic shipments by more than 40% within one quarter.

Customer concentration: The Company's ten largest customers accounted for 34% of fiscal 2024 revenue. The single largest customer, Apex Motors Assembly LLC, accounted for 9.1% of revenue.

Cybersecurity: PulseCare processes plant telemetry. A material breach of customer operational data could trigger contractual liabilities estimated by management at up to $85 million under current master service agreements.
