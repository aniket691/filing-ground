Note 5 - Net Sales

Net sales for the thirteen weeks ended April 30, 2025 were $2.186 billion, up 6.8% from $2.047 billion in the prior-year period. Comparable store sales increased 3.1%. E-commerce represented 29.4% of net sales versus 26.9% a year ago.

Gross margin was 34.8% in Q1 FY2025, down 70 basis points year over year, reflecting higher inbound freight costs and promotional activity in electronics.

Note 8 - Store Footprint

Cascade operated 438 stores as of April 30, 2025 (412 in the United States and 26 in Canada), versus 429 stores at January 31, 2025 after closing 9 underperforming locations and opening 18 new format stores.

Average selling square footage per store was 28,400. Capital expenditures in the quarter were $54 million, of which $31 million related to store remodels under the "Cascade Next" initiative.

Liquidity

Cash and cash equivalents were $318 million. The Company had $400 million of undrawn capacity under its revolving credit facility. Net debt was $892 million. Interest expense for the quarter was $11.7 million.

Outlook (Management Commentary)

Management expects full-year FY2025 net sales of $9.4 billion to $9.7 billion and operating margin of 7.5% to 8.0%, assuming freight unit costs stabilize after August 2025.
