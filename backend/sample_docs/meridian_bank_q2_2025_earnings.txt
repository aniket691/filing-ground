MERIDIAN COMMUNITY BANCORP
QUARTERLY EARNINGS RELEASE — SECOND QUARTER 2025
(Synthetic press release for RAG testing)

Austin, TX — July 18, 2025 — Meridian Community Bancorp ("Meridian" or "the Bank"), ticker MCBP, today reported results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

HEADLINE RESULTS

Net income for Q2 2025 was $87.4 million, or $1.12 per diluted share, compared with $71.9 million, or $0.93 per diluted share, in Q2 2024.

Net interest income was $246.8 million in Q2 2025, up 11.2% from $221.9 million in Q2 2024. Net interest margin (NIM) was 3.41%, compared with 3.18% a year earlier.

Noninterest income totaled $52.1 million, including $18.6 million of wealth-management fees and $9.4 million of mortgage banking revenue.

Provision for credit losses was $14.2 million, versus $19.5 million in Q2 2024.

BALANCE SHEET SNAPSHOT (June 30, 2025)

  - Total assets: $28.6 billion
  - Total loans (held for investment): $19.4 billion
  - Total deposits: $23.1 billion
  - Average loan yield: 6.08%
  - Average cost of interest-bearing deposits: 2.74%
  - Tangible common equity ratio: 8.9%
  - CET1 capital ratio: 11.4%

CREDIT QUALITY

Nonperforming assets were 0.62% of total assets. Net charge-offs were 0.28% of average loans (annualized). The allowance for credit losses stood at $287 million, or 1.48% of total loans.

SEGMENT COMMENTARY

Commercial and industrial loans grew 7.3% year over year to $8.2 billion. Residential mortgage loans were $6.1 billion. Consumer loans were $5.1 billion.

CEO QUOTE

"Our second-quarter NIM expansion to 3.41% and deposit franchise strength supported double-digit earnings growth," said CEO Priya Natarajan. "We remain focused on keeping CET1 above 11% while funding mid-single-digit loan growth."

GUIDANCE

For full-year 2025, Meridian reiterates net interest income guidance of $980 million to $1.02 billion and expects the effective tax rate to approximate 22.5%.

DIVIDEND

The Board declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.34 per share, payable August 15, 2025, to shareholders of record on August 1, 2025.
